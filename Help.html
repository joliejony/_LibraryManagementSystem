Help & FAQ

Quick answers about using the library catalogue and your account.

Holds

How do I place a hold?+

Open a book’s details page, choose a format (Book, eBook or Audiobook), then select Place Hold. For printed books, choose the branch where you want to pick it up. Your new hold appears under My Account → Items on hold with a “Waiting” status.

How will I know when my hold is ready?+

We email you when it’s your turn. The status in My Account changes from “Waiting” to “Ready for pickup”, and the pickup deadline is shown beside it.

How long do I have to pick up a hold?+

Ready holds are kept at the pickup branch for 7 days. After the “Pick up by” date, the item goes to the next reader in line.

Do eBooks and audiobooks need a pickup branch?+

No. Digital holds are borrowed online, so there’s nothing to pick up. We email you when the title is ready to read or listen to.

How do I cancel a hold?+

Go to My Account → Items on hold and select Cancel hold beside the item.

Borrowing & renewing

How do I renew a book?+

In My Account → Borrowed items, select Renew item, or Renew all to renew every eligible item at once.

Why can’t I renew an item?+

An item can’t be renewed when another reader has placed a hold on it. Please return it by the due date so the next reader can borrow it.

Events

Do I need to register for events?+

Each event card shows a tag: Registration required, Registration recommended or Drop-in. Drop-in events need no sign-up.

How do I see or cancel my event registrations?+

Your registrations are listed in My Account → My events. Select Cancel registration to free up your spot for someone else.

Your account

I forgot my password. What do I do?+

On the Log in page, select Forgot password?, enter the email on your demo account, then choose a new password. It is saved in this browser; no reset email is sent.

Can I change or remove a rating?+

Yes. On the book’s details page, select Edit rating to choose a new rating or remove yours.
